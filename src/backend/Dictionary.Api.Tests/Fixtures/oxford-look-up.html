Definition of look up phrasal verb from the Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary

look up

phrasal verb
jump to other results
look up
  1. (informal) (of business, somebody’s situation, etc.) to become better synonym improve
    • At last things were beginning to look up.

    Want to learn more?

    Find out which words work together and produce more natural sounding English with the Oxford Collocations Dictionary app.

look up (from something)
  1. to raise your eyes when you are looking down at something
    • She looked up from her book as I entered the room.
    • He looked up at me sharply when I said that.
look somebody up
  1. [no passive] (informal) to visit or make contact with somebody, especially when you have not seen them for a long time
    • Do look me up the next time you're in London.
look something up
 
  1. to look for information in a dictionary or reference book, or by using a computer
    • Can you look up the opening times on the website?
    • I looked it up in the dictionary.
